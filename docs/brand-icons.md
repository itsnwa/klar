# Brand icons

The brand icons you can give a group or space in Klar are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.38.1, written by `scripts/update_brand_icons.py`. Simple Icons releases its work under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Some icons are available under a license of their own, listed below; "Not listed" means Simple Icons has no license data for that icon.

All brand names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners. Klar shows them so you can mark a group or space with a brand you know, which doesn't imply any affiliation with or endorsement by their owners. If you own one of these brands and want its icon removed, please open an issue.
